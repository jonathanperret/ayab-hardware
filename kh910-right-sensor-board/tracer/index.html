Nets

Move pads: drag a circle onto its real pad, in each photo separately. A pad you've dragged turns green on that side.
Connect (draw a trace): click the start pad, click along the copper to add corners, click the end pad. The trace belongs to the photo you draw it in; the other photo shows it faded. ⌫ removes the last corner, Esc cancels.
Edit a trace: click it, then drag its yellow corners; double-click on the trace to add a corner, double-click a corner to remove it.
Place part: click the part's pads in pin order (pin 1 first; for resistors either order), type its reference from the silkscreen, Enter. Click a part outline to edit it.
Add pad: click where a pad is missing; place it in the other photo too.
Drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom. Clicking a pad centres both photos on it. Esc deselect, ⌘Z undo.
Dashed link = unsure, solid = confirmed. Saves automatically.
